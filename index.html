StreamFlow Forecast

forecasting

Pulls live USGS streamflow sensor data via the Hydrofunctions module and plots nine years of historical river flow against a current-year, week-long forecast — surfacing the highest-flow year, lowest-flow year, and a "typical" flow band for comparison.
